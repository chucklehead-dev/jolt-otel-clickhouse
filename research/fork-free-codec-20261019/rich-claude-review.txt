## Review: fork-free codec + explicit-codec row adapter (read-only)

No defects found that meet the bar (correctness bugs in bounds/ownership/order/UTF-8/numbers, default-behavior regressions, vacuous controls, or misleading comparisons). Findings below are confirmatory, with two minor non-blocking observations.

**traversal.ss** — `ffc-pmap-fold`'s flat-vector branch strides correctly by 2; the hnode branch recurses into children and reverses `hcoll-alist` via `backward` to restore stock seq order for collision buckets. `probe.clj`'s forced-collision cases ("Aa"/"BB"/"AaAa"/"BBBB") confirm visit order against `(mapv vec m)`, including a case asserting the root is genuinely a single-bucket `hcoll` (lines 42-48) — a real check, not vacuous. `ffc-stock-sorted-map?` gates on `htable-sorted-map?` plus identity-equality of `:ops` against the trusted `sm-ops` var-cell root; `oracle.clj` lines 63-66 swap `:ops` to `empty-pmap` and assert rejection — the one place a tampered sorted map could silently mis-traverse, and it's covered. The in-order walk never calls the comparator; `oracle.clj` lines 56-62 arm a comparator to throw post-construction and show output still matches comparator-implied order — a strong, non-vacuous check.

**json.ss** — The int64/uint64 digit-scratch fast path is correct at both ends by hand-trace (20-digit max, 19-digit negative min), both exercised directly in `oracle.clj` along with ±1-outside-range bignum fallback. UTF-8 encoding is correct for 1–4 byte sequences and surrogate pairs; lone surrogates are rejected (unreachable from an ordinary Chez string, which the file notes and `oracle.clj` confirms via `integer->char` rejection — correctly scoped as an input-boundary check, not an encoder-reachability claim). `string!`'s unchecked ASCII cursor only runs under a `(string? x)` gate within hand-verified bounds; the chunk-boundary interaction (publish-before-grow, publish-before-escape) is specifically exercised by `oracle.clj`'s boundary sweeps at exact 64 KiB edges with multi-byte/escaped tails straddling them — the strongest evidence against an off-by-one in the ASCII-cursor design. Geometric chunk growth (1 KiB scalar / 64 KiB lines, doubling, capped at 64 KiB and by remaining budget) is verified at exact limits (`[1 2]` at limit 4 rejects, at 5 accepts, plus larger string sweeps). The shared per-invocation `container-depth` mutable is safe under the strictly nested, synchronous recursion here; the `[[[1]]]` vs `max-depth 1` case confirms the off-by-one depth check fires at the right level. Ownership is real: `make-jolt-array` always builds a fresh contiguous array, and `oracle.clj` mutates one returned array and asserts the other is unaffected — a genuine anti-aliasing check. The projected-row callback resets depth to 0 per row and calls `project` once per record; failure-isolation and nested-encode-inside-callback are both covered.

**check-oracle.bb / probe.clj** — Legitimate independent-process oracle (Babashka + stock Cheshire) over 180 synthetic cases (non-BMP text, control chars, ±0.0, extreme doubles, 64-bit boundaries, UTF-8-literal and `\u`-escaped variants), reusing neither `traversal.ss` nor `json.ss`.
