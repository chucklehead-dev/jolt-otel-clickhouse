Reviewed the five new diagnostic files plus the one-line erratum in `research/matched-native-floor-20261019/README.md`. Findings:

**1. Calibration clue mixes two incompatible counter systems (main finding).**
`README.md:35-40` explains the observed-vs-plain delta of ~2241 B/row as "consistent with two snapshots for each of seven observed operations/row (2*7*160=2240)". But the 2241 B/row figure comes from the *whole-batch* `:estimated-bytes` summary, which `screen.clj:8-9,48-49` computes from `(host/bytes-allocated)+(host/gc-bytes)` — the host/live-heap counter. The 160 B/call figure comes from a completely different counter: Chez's `sstats-bytes`, measured inside `probe.ss:20` and aggregated in the noop calibration (`screen.edn` `:noop-calibration [35000 17356584 5600000 17072963 0]`, 5,600,000/35,000=160). The same README's own "Counter semantics" section (lines 50-53) explicitly warns these two counters are different and shouldn't be conflated ("`sstats-bytes` ... unlike the public `jolt.host/bytes-allocated` live-heap reading"), yet the calibration paragraph does exactly that. The numeric near-match (2241≈2240) is therefore not confirmatory — it's two unrelated-unit numbers that happen to multiply out close to each other. Separately, even taken on its own terms the arithmetic needs an unexplained ×2: a wrapped call already includes both the before/after snapshot pair (that's what produces the 160 B/call in the noop test), so 7 wrapped calls/row should predict ~7×160=1120 B/row, not 2×7×160=2240. Smallest fix: either drop the specific "2*7*160=2240" formula, or add one sentence noting it mixes host and sstats counters and the agreement is coincidental/unverified, not a derivation.
