For RowBinary, the whole 5000-row payload rides inside the single `execute-owned-and-flush!` call — it *is* the data transfer, not just a confirm (one native touch per run, carrying all the bytes). So RowBinary's lower native-call count is partly a transport-shape difference (single combined call vs. streamed write + separate flush), not purely a wire-format effect. Neither the `jdbc.chdb.native`/`jdbc.chdb.durable` internals nor the one product file given as context expose whether `chdb-stream-insert-n` itself invokes a flush internally, so whether this is "1 vs 1" or "1 vs 2" logical round trips can't be settled from the reviewed files — flag this before attributing timing deltas to format alone.
